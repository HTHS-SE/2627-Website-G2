VEX RECF




The Robotics Education & Competition Foundation (RECF) competition is a global competition open to students of all ages run by the RECF. This competition used to be in collaboration with VEX, but has since split, allowing students to use parts besides VEX parts in their builds. Students in elementary and middle school compete with VEX IQ parts, this year competing in the Tier Takeover competition. Students in middle and high school compete with VEX V5 parts, competing in the Pinnacle V2.0 competition this year.
